## GL Transitions

`transitions/gl/` and the `gl-*` items in `components/` are converted from
[gl-transitions/gl-transitions](https://github.com/gl-transitions/gl-transitions).
Each file names its original transition, author and license on its first line and keeps the
original header. Most are MIT ([licenses/gl-transitions-MIT.txt](licenses/gl-transitions-MIT.txt));
`gl-inverted-page-curl` (Hewlett-Packard) is BSD 3-Clause and `gl-stereo-viewer`
(Ted Schundler) is BSD 2-Clause, with their full terms kept in the file.

## HyperShader

`vendor/hyper-shader/hyper-shader.js` and the `hyper-shader` item are built from
`@hyperframes/shader-transitions` (HyperFrames, HeyGen) with Glintmotion's `registerShader`
addition, under the Apache License 2.0 ([licenses/hyperframes-Apache-2.0.txt](licenses/hyperframes-Apache-2.0.txt)).
`vendor/hyper-shader/source.json` records the package version and the Glintmotion commit.
